The user asks for the command to show (stale) processes. They want the command I used: Get-CimInstance Win32_Process filtering python.exe, showing ProcessId, ParentProcessId, CommandLine.
Let me give a concise command. Maybe also to identify the shop/admin servers.
Keep it short.
```powershell
Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name='python.exe' OR Name='pythonw.exe'" |
  Select-Object ProcessId, ParentProcessId, CommandLine | Format-List

To spot just the shop/admin servers:

```powershell
Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name='python.exe'" |
  Where-Object { $_.CommandLine -match 'shop\.exe|admin\.exe|uvicorn|app\.main|app\.admin' } |
  Select-Object ProcessId, ParentProcessId, CommandLine | Format-Table -Wrap

Or by listening port:
Get-NetTCPConnection -State Listen | Where-Object LocalPort -in 9000,9100 |
  Select-Object LocalPort, OwningProcess
